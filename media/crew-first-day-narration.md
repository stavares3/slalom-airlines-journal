# Narration script: The first line check

Read by Windows SAPI text to speech (Microsoft Zira Desktop, rate -1) and
committed beside the rendered `crew-first-day.mp3`. This audio is synthesized
demo narration for a fictional airline; the player and the article metadata
both label it as such.

---

The Journal, from Slalom Airlines. The first line check, written by Elena Marsh. This is a synthesized demo narration.

Every new member of our cabin crew flies their first working trip with an examiner on board. We call it the line check. Mine was a Seattle to Denver turn on a Thursday in June.

I arrived ninety minutes early, which the examiner noted, and not as a compliment. Boarding is a river. One hundred and sixty people, each carrying a bag and a mood, and your work is to read both without appearing to read either.

Over the Rockies we hit ninety seconds of honest chop during the coffee run. Cups capped, cart braked, a hand on the seatback, and a word for the nervous flyer in seventeen C, all of it done before I had decided to do any of it. Training waits inside you until the airplane asks for it.

We landed four minutes early. The examiner signed my paperwork and gave me the two lines I keep taped inside my locker. Slow is smooth. And the passengers are not interrupting the job. They are the job.

This has been The Journal, from Slalom Airlines. Fly the world.
